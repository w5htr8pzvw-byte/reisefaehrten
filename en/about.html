About me

I'm Niclas. I've been taking photographs for a long time, but it was travelling that turned it into more than a hobby: the wish to capture individual moments in a way that still means something years later.

Reisefährten is the collection of images where that actually worked — chosen deliberately, not just anything, and meant to be printed, not just filed away.
