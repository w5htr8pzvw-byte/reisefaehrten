2. Hosting via Netlify

This website is hosted via Netlify (Netlify, Inc.). When the site is accessed, Netlify automatically processes technical data such as the IP address in server log files, among other things to deliver the page and protect against abuse. Details: Netlify Privacy Policy.

3. Contact form

The contact form is processed via Netlify's built-in "Netlify Forms" feature (no separate third-party service). The data entered (name, email, message) is transmitted to Netlify and stored in its form inbox, or forwarded via email notification. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1)(b) GDPR (processing a request). See the Netlify Privacy Policy for details (section 2). A hidden extra field serves solely as spam protection (honeypot) and is not evaluated when left blank.

4. Google Fonts

This site loads the "Fraunces" and "Work Sans" typefaces via Google Fonts; the font files are loaded from Google's servers when the page is opened, which can transmit the visitor's IP address to Google. Note: for a site aimed mainly at German visitors, it is more privacy-friendly to self-host Google Fonts instead (download the files and include them via css/fonts/) to avoid this data transfer.
